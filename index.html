Scoring Rules
How It Works

Every discipline contributes equally to the final lab ranking. The maximum points a lab can earn is 100 per discipline, regardless of whether it is an individual or team event. Points follow a non-linear curve: winning is dramatically rewarded, but every finishing position still earns something — last place always scores zero.

Individual Disciplines (01–04)

Each lab enters 2 athletes who compete individually against all 16 athletes. The lab score is the sum of both athletes' points.

🧠 Consistency matters: 2nd + 4th (45 + 33 = 78 pts) beats 1st + 10th (55 + 16 = 71 pts).

Tiebreakers

If two labs finish on the same total points:

  1. Most 1st-place finishes across all disciplines
  2. Most 2nd-place finishes across all disciplines
  3. Head-to-head result in the last discipline of the week (Flunky Ball)
