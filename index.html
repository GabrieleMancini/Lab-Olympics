The Spirit of the Games
More Than Competition

The Lab Olympics is a space to compete, but above all to connect, with people from other labs who share the same daily life, the same passions, the same frustrations.

🤝
No discrimination, ever

Everyone is welcome!

😄
Have fun

Medals are great; a good time is better.

👐
Bring everyone along

Don't pick your lineup always based on who's the best athlete, make sure everyone gets to play.
