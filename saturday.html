Lab Olympics 2026 · Cortex United
Saturday
June 6 · Alster Lake

🚲 Bike · 🔁 Staffel · 🏃 Run

At a glance
Key Info
📍
Meeting Point
Alster Lake shore near the race START pin. Arrive by 14:00 — briefing starts immediately. First gun at 14:15. Everyone should be done by ~16:15.
🚲
Stadtrad Bikes
Two Stadtrad stations are marked on the map. Individual bikers start at the first station; the Staffel handoff happens at the second.
👟
The Route
Approx. 7.5 km lap around the Alster Lake. Bikers go first, then runners take over from the same starting line.
Programme
Detailed Schedule
14:00 All athletes
🤝 Meeting & Briefing
Everyone gathers at the START point on the Alster shore. Captains confirm participant lists, officials do a quick briefing on the route, rules, and timing. ~15 min.
All teams 📍 START pin
14:15 Individual Bike Race
🚲 Individual Bike Start
Each team's solo cyclist picks up a Stadtrad bike at Station #1 (blue pin) and rides the ~7.5 km Alster loop. Rolling start — up to 5 min window to grab bikes and go, avoiding a bottleneck at the dock. Timing stops when you return the bike.
1 member per team 🚲 Stadtrad Station #1 ~7.5 km · ~30 min
14:50 Staffel Race
🔁 Staffel Start — Bike Leg
The Staffel relay kicks off. By now most individual bikers have departed and the station is free. The first member bikes from Station #1, rides roughly the first half of the loop to Station #2 (purple pin, ~15 min), parks the bike, and tags their partner.
2 members per team Leg 1 — Bike ~15 min Leg 2 — Run ~25 min
↳ ~16:05 Staffel Handoff
🤝 Staffel Changeover at Station #2
The biker parks at Stadtrad Station #2 (purple pin) and tags the runner, who sprints the remaining half of the loop back to the finish. Staffel teams expected to finish around ~15:30.
📍 Stadtrad Station #2
15:15 Individual Run
🏃 Individual Running Race Start
All solo runners line up at the START and tackle the full ~7.5 km Alster loop on foot. Individual bikers are all finished by now. Timing starts at the gun and stops at the finish line. Last runners expected to finish around ~16:05.
1 member per team 📍 START pin ~7.5 km · ~50 min
